01 — The vibe

A calm corner that keeps late hours.

Tucked into Zubairah Al Roumiah, Savva is the kind of place people come back to for the quiet — comfy seats indoors and out, unhurried mornings, and coffee that guests describe again and again as balanced: never too bitter, never too sweet.

Pick your bean, Ethiopian or Colombian, and let the rest of the day slow down. Open from sunrise past midnight, every day but Friday afternoon.
